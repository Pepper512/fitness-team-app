Connect your coach

🔒 Your key is stored only on this device (your browser's local storage) and is sent straight to Anthropic — it's never in the app's code and never goes to anyone else. For personal use this is the simplest safe setup. If you ever shared this app with others, you'd route calls through a small server instead. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

Whoop — recovery-aware coaching

One-time setup is in whoop-proxy/WHOOP-SETUP.md. Your Whoop secret lives in the Worker; only a rotating refresh token is stored on this device. Once connected, today's recovery/sleep/HRV show on the Plan tab and the coach adjusts your sessions to it.
